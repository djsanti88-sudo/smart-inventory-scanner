## What the live run taught us (already fixed + committed)

1. Product URLs are `/buy-tires/<slug>/p/<digits>` on www.discounttire.com; catalog
   sitemap is `sitemaps.discounttire.com/sitemap_full_product.xml` -> one .gz child,
   18,950 urls, of which **7,886 are tire product pages** (`9ec0690`).
2. DT's JSON-LD Product block has **no GTIN**. The barcode + full specs arrive via the
   page's own `webapi/discounttire.graph?op=productByCode` response; the fetcher now
   captures that from natural page traffic - no extra requests (`70b6f1b`).
3. Playwright `waitForSelector` on `<script>` tags needs `state: "attached"` (`70b6f1b`).

## Guard rejections (poison guard working as designed)

4 pages (Interco ATV, 2x Cooper Evolution Winter Studded, Hankook i*Pike studded) carry
DT's internal article code in the `gtin` field (e.g. "36954" = the /p/ code) instead of
a real GS1 barcode. The check-digit guard rejected all 4; none can poison the corpus.
Studded/ATV variants apparently lack real retail GTINs at DT.
