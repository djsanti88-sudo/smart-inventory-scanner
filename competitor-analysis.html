What to charge

Flat pricing — no per-seat tax, no mandatory onboarding fee. Start at $39; $99–$299 unlocks as Tier A/B ships.

Bottom line

Ship the brain you already built. Lead with “every code, learned once — counts that never double.” Price it $0 / $39 / $99 / $299.

We have the smartest engine in SMB barcode inventory and no one markets it — real white space. Competitors don't beat us on intelligence; they beat us on existing in production. Win the wedge they're all bad at, then add the table-stakes integrations to reach their shortlists.
