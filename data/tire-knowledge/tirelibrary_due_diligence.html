⚠️ The deciding factor — the data license

Their public Terms of Use state the material is for "personal use only and may not be re-sold or re-distributed," and grant permission for "personal, non-commercial use." Two problems for us:

  • Commercial use: the Smart Inventory Scanner is a commercial product (future SaaS). Embedding their data could violate the personal/non-commercial clause.
  • Keeping it after cancel: licensed data is normally usable only while subscribed. "Pay one month, keep forever" would be using the data past the license term.

BUT: the $250 API tier is explicitly sold for developer integration — i.e., a commercial product — so it almost certainly carries a separate commercial data-license agreement that overrides the generic website terms. That agreement isn't public. This is the single thing to nail down before paying.

5. Before you pay — ask them these (legal@esprofessionals.com / sales)

  • Does the $250 API plan allow commercial use of the data in my own inventory/scanner application?
  • After I cancel, may I keep and keep using the data I downloaded, or must I stop/delete it?
  • Is the full 240k catalog included in the CSV flat-file feed (no per-record or volume cap)?
  • Any redistribution limits if the scanner is later sold to other shops (multi-tenant SaaS)?
  • Is the API/feed license month-to-month with true cancel-anytime, or any minimum commitment?

6. Recommendation

  • If they confirm commercial use + the right to keep the data → $250 one-time is a strong buy: 240k validated tire barcodes for ~$250 beats months of free trickle, and it's 100% clean (paying their real bulk price, using the sanctioned API).
  • If they say personal-use-only / must-delete-on-cancel → don't; it can't legitimately power your product, and the free daily engine remains your clean path.
  • Smart move: start the 7-day free trial of the API tier, confirm the license in writing first, and only then pull + pay. The trial lets you verify the data quality and the CSV format at zero cost.
