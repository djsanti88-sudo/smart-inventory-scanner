# CompetitionBot — competitor analysis

Research method: public web/search only (no logins, no paywalls, no private scraping, no wholesale copying).
Pricing cited below is public list pricing as found; where not public it is marked "not public." Full grid:
`competitor_matrix.csv`.

## The real competitive set
SIS competes most directly with **Sortly** (mobile barcode inventory for SMBs). **inFlow** and **Zoho
Inventory** are heavier order/ERP systems — they out-feature SIS on orders/accounting but are overkill and
pricey for a shop that mainly needs to *count* accurately. **Tireweb / TyresAddict** are tire *data*
sources, not apps — potential SIS suppliers/add-ons, not rivals.

## Public pricing (cited)
- **Sortly** — Free (100 items, 1 user); Advanced **$49/mo**; Ultra **$149/mo**; Premium **$299/mo**;
  Enterprise custom. [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/169199/Sortly-Pro/) · [Sortly](https://www.sortly.com/blog/how-much-does-inventory-software-cost/)
- **inFlow Inventory** — Entrepreneur **$186/mo**; Small Business **$436/mo**; Mid-Size **$999/mo** (annual ~20% off).
  [SoftwareAdvice](https://www.softwareadvice.com/scm/inflow-inventory-profile/) · [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/operations-management-software/a/inflow-inventory/)
- **Zoho Inventory** — Free (50 orders/mo); Standard **$39**; Professional **$99**; Premium **$159**;
  Enterprise **$299** per org/mo (annual cheaper). [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/146241/Zoho-Inventory/pricing/)
- **Tireweb Library** — ~240k tires by UPC/EAN/GTIN; **pricing not public** (commercial license). [Tireweb](https://www.tireweb.com/product/tireweb-library)
- **TyresAddict / TiresAddict** — tire DB (SQL/CSV); **pricing not public**. [TyresAddict](https://tyresaddict.com/help/databases/)

## What competitors do better
- Sortly: polished native mobile apps, photos, QuickBooks integration, brand trust, a true free tier.
- inFlow/Zoho: full orders/POs/purchasing/accounting/multichannel + integrations.
- All three: mature reporting dashboards and onboarding/marketing.

## What SIS does better (defensible)
- **Separator-insensitive multi-code resolution** — scan a tire's barcode OR its part number (dashed/
  spaced/slashed) → same product. Generic tools don't normalize like this.
- **Wrong-category mismatch guard** — stops the "tire → cigarettes" class of human error. Unique trust feature.
- **Niche fit** for tire/auto/parts counting + a **private code knowledge base** the shop builds (a moat
  that compounds), kept non-extractable by customers (after the P0 fix).

## Conceptually copy (don't clone text/UI)
Sortly's onboarding simplicity + free tier as a funnel; Zoho's clean tiered pricing page; QuickBooks
export as a near-term integration; photos/library polish.

## Avoid
Don't chase inFlow/Zoho's ERP/order scope — it dilutes the "fast accurate counting" wedge and raises support cost.

## Could become a paid add-on for us
Licensed **tire catalog enrichment** (Tireweb/TyresAddict as a supplier) → "tire library" premium add-on,
turning a competitor data source into our upsell.

## Honest gaps in this research
Sortly/inFlow/Zoho list prices are public; exact current promos and feature-by-tier details change — verify
on their pricing pages before quoting to a customer. Tire-DB pricing is not public (requires a sales contact).
